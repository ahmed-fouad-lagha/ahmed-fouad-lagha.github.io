I am an AI/ML Engineer and PhD Researcher in the Department of Data Science and Engineering at Eötvös Loránd University (ELTE) in Budapest, Hungary. My work spans production ML and LLM systems, privacy-preserving machine learning, and retrieval-augmented generation. I build and ship end-to-end systems (e.g. Vellum, a production AI platform) while also advancing privacy-preserving methods (DP-SGD, synthetic data) in first-author research. I aim to bridge rigorous theoretical foundations with robust, scalable software engineering that can safely operate in high-stakes, sensitive environments.

With a strong academic background in mathematics and computer science, I focus on taking ideas from first principles all the way to dependable, production-ready code. I am always keen to connect with fellow researchers and engineers working on private AI, applied machine learning, and system security.
