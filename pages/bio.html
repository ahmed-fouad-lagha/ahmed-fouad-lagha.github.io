I am an AI/ML Engineer and PhD Researcher in the Department of Data Science and Engineering at Eötvös Loránd University (ELTE) in Budapest, Hungary. My doctoral studies are supported by the Hungarian Government's Stipendium Hungaricum Scholarship.

My interests sit at the intersection of machine learning, data privacy, and security. I am broadly fascinated by synthetic data generation, differential privacy, and building trustworthy AI systems that can be safely deployed in high-stakes, sensitive environments. My work is driven by bridging rigorous theoretical foundations with robust, scalable software engineering.

With a strong academic background in mathematics and computer science, I focus on taking ideas from first principles all the way to dependable, production-ready code. I am always keen to connect with fellow researchers and engineers working on private AI, applied machine learning, and system security.
