I am a PhD Candidate in Computer Science at Eötvös Loránd University (ELTE), where my work focuses on synthetic data generation, privacy-preserving machine learning, and trustworthy AI systems.

My research sits at the intersection of machine learning, privacy, and security, with a particular interest in differential privacy, generative models, and privacy-aware data analysis pipelines that preserve utility without exposing sensitive information.

I am motivated by building robust AI systems that are both useful and responsible, with strong attention to real-world deployment, evaluation, and societal impact.
