# Teaching & Mentorship

I design practical learning experiences in machine learning security, privacy, and trustworthy AI. My teaching focuses on helping students connect technical foundations with realistic security and privacy challenges.

## IDaSec Practicum

I authored and maintain the [IDaSec Practicum](https://elte-dsed.github.io/Intro-Data-Security/), an introduction to data security at Eötvös Loránd University. The practicum contains 13 hands-on labs across 8 modules covering:

* Attacking and defending machine-learning models
* Privacy risks and differential privacy
* Security evaluation of data-driven systems
* Practical auditing and reproducible experimentation

The course materials are openly available and supported by a GitHub Actions workflow that builds and deploys the documentation site on every merge to `main`.

## Student Mentorship

I have supported B.Sc. and M.Sc. students working on secure machine learning, differential privacy, and thesis research through technical guidance, project discussions, and feedback on research design.
